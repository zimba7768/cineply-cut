# Cineply Cut

A free, open-source desktop video editor for Windows: a multi-track timeline, standard and extra transitions, title pages, full audio editing, and built-in Higgsfield AI generation.

> 🚧 **Work in progress.** The first version is under active development.

## Planned features

- **Multi-track timeline**: unlimited video and audio tracks, with razor, ripple delete, snapping, trimming, undo/redo
- **Transitions**: cross dissolve, dip to black/white, wipes, slides/push, zoom, iris, blur, spin, glitch, and more
- **Titles**: title pages and lower thirds with templates, fonts, backgrounds, and text animations
- **Audio editing**: waveforms, volume keyframes, fades, pan, mute/solo, EQ, compressor, reverb, delay, normalize, noise reduction, voiceover recording
- **Higgsfield AI**: text-to-video, image-to-video, text-to-image, and AI voice, with results imported straight into the media bin
- **Export**: MP4 (H.264) at 1080p or 4K, vertical 9:16 presets, and audio-only WAV/MP3

## Tech

Electron, React, and TypeScript, with a bundled FFmpeg.
